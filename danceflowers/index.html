Good to know

Details

How far ahead should I order?

A week is ideal. A few days usually works. Dance weekends fill up, so earlier is better.

Where do I pick up?

Idaho Falls. I'll send you the address and a time window when I confirm your order.

Can you match my dress?

Yes. Send a photo of the dress or a swatch and I'll match the flowers and ribbon to it.

How do I keep it fresh?

Keep it in the fridge in the box it comes in until you're ready to leave. It'll look great through the last song.

Group orders?

Absolutely. Ordering for a whole friend group or a wedding party is welcome. Put everyone's colors in the notes.

How do I pay?

Venmo when you order is easiest, and the confirmation gives you the link. Cash at pickup is fine too. Flowers are paid for by pickup, no deposit needed.

What if plans change?

Text me at 208-403-7323 as early as you can. I buy fresh flowers a few days before the dance, so a heads up helps more than anything.
